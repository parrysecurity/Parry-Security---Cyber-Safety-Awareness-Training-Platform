Bad Password Habits to Break

What NOT to do with your passwords

  • 🔐
    Using "password", "123456", or "qwerty"These are the first passwords attackers try — crackable in seconds
  • 🔄
    Reusing passwords across accountsOne breached account compromises all your others
  • 📝
    Writing passwords on sticky notesAnyone who sees your desk has access to your accounts
  • 🎂
    Using personal info (birthdays, pet names)Easily found on social media — attackers research you
  • 🔓
    Sharing passwords via email or textUnencrypted messages can be intercepted
  • ⏰
    Never changing default passwordsDefault credentials are publicly documented for most devices
✅

Strong Password Habits

What you SHOULD do instead

  • 🔐
    Use a Password ManagerGenerates and stores unique, complex passwords for every account
  • 🔢
    Create long passphrases (12+ characters)"Correct-Horse-Battery-Staple" is memorable and strong
  • 🔄
    Use different passwords for every accountBreach of one service doesn't endanger others
  • 🔒
    Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)Adds a second layer of protection beyond your password
  • 🔄
    Change passwords after security incidentsWhen a service announces a breach, update immediately
  • 🔍
    Check if your passwords are compromisedUse sites like HaveIBeenPwned to check for breaches

Glossary of Terms

Key concepts explained — click to expand

Password Manager+
A secure application that generates, stores, and autofills unique, complex passwords for all your accounts. Only one master password to remember.
Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)+
A security layer requiring two forms of verification: something you know (password) and something you have (code from phone or hardware key).
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)+
Similar to 2FA but can include multiple verification methods like biometrics (fingerprint/face ID).
Credential Stuffing+
Automated attack where hackers use stolen username/password pairs from one breach to try logging into other services.
Brute Force Attack+
Attackers try every possible password combination. Long passwords (12+ characters) make this practically impossible.
Authenticator App+
An app that generates time-based one-time passwords (TOTP) for 2FA without requiring SMS.
