Public Wi‑Fi Dangers

Why "Free Wi‑Fi" can be expensive

  • 👁️
    Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) Attacks Attackers intercept data between your device and the network — they see everything you send
  • 🎭
    Evil Twin Hotspots Fake networks that look legitimate ("Free Airport Wi-Fi") — designed to steal your credentials
  • 🔓
    Unencrypted Networks Most public Wi‑Fi lacks encryption, making your traffic readable to anyone on the same network
  • 📡
    Session Hijacking Attackers steal your login session cookies to access your accounts without a password
  • 🦠
    Malware Distribution Compromised networks can inject malware into websites you visit or downloads
📱

Mobile Security Threats

Your phone is a target too

  • 📲
    Malicious Apps Fake apps that steal data, show intrusive ads, or install malware on your device
  • 🔐
    Over-Permissioned Apps Flashlight apps requesting contacts, location, and camera access — why?
  • 📡
    Bluetooth Vulnerabilities Leaving Bluetooth on exposes your device to nearby attacks in public places
  • 📱
    SIM Swapping Attackers trick your carrier into moving your phone number to their SIM card
  • 📧
    Smishing (SMS Phishing) Fake text messages with malicious links — often about package deliveries or bank alerts

How to Stay Safe on Public Wi‑Fi & Mobile

9 essential security practices

🔒
Always use a VPN on public Wi‑Fi Encrypts your traffic — choose a reputable VPN provider
📶
Use mobile hotspot instead of public Wi‑Fi Your phone's cellular data is much more secure
🔐
Verify the network name Ask staff for the official Wi‑Fi name before connecting
📱
Keep your phone updated iOS and Android security patches fix known vulnerabilities
📲
Review app permissions Does Solitaire need access to your contacts and location?
🔑
Use biometric locks Face ID or fingerprint — much stronger than a simple passcode
📡
Turn off auto-connect Disable automatic connection to open networks in Wi‑Fi settings
🔊
Disable Bluetooth when not in use Reduces attack surface for nearby attackers
💾
Enable remote wipe Find My iPhone or Find My Device can erase your phone if stolen

Glossary of Terms

Key concepts explained — click to expand

VPN (Virtual Private Network)+
Encrypts your internet traffic and hides your IP address, protecting your data on public Wi‑Fi networks. Essential for secure browsing away from home.
Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) Attack+
Attackers secretly intercept and potentially alter communication between two parties. Common on unencrypted public Wi‑Fi networks.
Evil Twin Attack+
A fake Wi‑Fi hotspot that mimics a legitimate one. Users connect thinking it's safe, but attackers capture all their traffic.
Smishing (SMS Phishing)+
Phishing attacks conducted via text message, often disguised as package delivery alerts, bank fraud warnings, or prize notifications.
SIM Swapping+
Attackers trick your mobile carrier into transferring your phone number to their SIM card, allowing them to intercept 2FA codes and take over accounts.
HTTPS+
A secure version of HTTP that encrypts data between your browser and website. Look for the padlock icon in your address bar.
